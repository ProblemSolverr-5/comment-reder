> **Instant, zero-install YouTube performance analytics and AI-powered comment sentiment intelligence for any public video.**

## 🌟 Overview

Designed for content creators, digital marketers, and researchers to analyze YouTube video performance and audience feedback in seconds. 

**The Best Part:** You do not need to be the owner of the channel! Simply copy and paste any public YouTube video link into the platform to gain immediate, actionable insights—without needing any app downloads or installations.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📈 1. Core Performance Analytics
* **Vital Metrics:** Real-time extraction of total views, overall likes, total comments, and upload date.
* **Engagement Trends:** Quick breakdown of audience engagement ratios and view velocity.

### 🧠 2. Smart Emotion & Sentiment Filtering
Using Natural Language Processing (NLP), comments are automatically categorized into actionable segments:
* 🙂 **Positive / Happy:** Audience appreciation, praise, and supportive reactions.
* 🙁 **Critical / Constructive:** Viewer complaints, constructive criticism, and pain points.
* ❓ **Viewer Questions:** Queries from the audience to inspire future content ideas.
* 💡 **Suggestions & Feedback:** Feature requests, tips, and recommendations from viewers.
* 😐 **Neutral Remarks:** General observations and context-based remarks.

### 🔍 3. Deep Engagement Insights
* **Top-Liked Comments:** Instantly surface high-impact comments that resonate most with the audience.
* **Keyword & Topic Extraction:** Identify repeating phrases, recurring themes, and trending keywords.
* **Active Discussion Threads:** Highlight long-running threads and high-reply conversations.

---

## 🚀 How It Works

1. **Paste Link:** Enter any valid public YouTube video URL.
2. **Fetch & Analyze:** The platform processes the video metadata and parses comments through the NLP sentiment engine.
3. **Explore Dashboard:** Review visual metrics, sentiment breakdowns, and exportable audience insights.

We are actively developing new features to make this tool even more powerful. Here is what is coming next:
